I'd like to believe that I can help you get your way around any problem that you're trying to solve through computers (and more specifically, programming).

I may not know every skill there is out in the world, but I believe the most important skill that any Computer Scientist can have is the ability to learn. Thus, even if I don't know much about the problem you're currently dealing, it'd be my pleasure (and excitement!) for me to be able to learn more about what you're trying to solve, and brainstorm the best ways to go about it.

Fundamentally, I'd like to add value to whatever it is you're trying to do; if I believe I can't add value, or you'd have to invest more effort in bringing me up to speed than the value I'd bring in, I'll tell you frankly so (as this would be a very frustrating experience for both of us). Anyhow, I love collaborating with others and working in teams; if you'd like to work with me or simply have a chat, feel free to reach out to me!
